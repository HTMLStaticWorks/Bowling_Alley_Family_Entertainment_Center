5 Tips to Improve Your Bowling Score Instantly

Whether you're throwing gutters or just missing that spare, these 5 simple tips will help you hit the pocket consistently and boost your average score.

1. Choose the Right Ball Weight

One of the most common mistakes beginners make is grabbing a ball that is either too heavy or too light. A general rule of thumb is that your ball should weigh about 10% of your body weight (up to the maximum 16 lbs). You should be able to swing it freely without straining your arm.

2. Focus on the Arrows, Not the Pins

Don't look at the pins when you release the ball! Look at the arrows on the lane. The lane is designed to help you aim. For a right-handed bowler, aiming for the second arrow from the right is usually a great starting point for a strike.

"Bowling is not about power; it's about consistency, timing, and accuracy. Master your approach, and the pins will fall."

3. Keep Your Wrist Straight

A straight wrist transfers more power to the ball and gives you better control. If your wrist bends backward during your swing, you'll lose hook potential and accuracy.

4. Find Your Starting Position

Consistency starts before you even move. Find a specific dot on the approach area and stand there every single time. If your ball hits too far to the right, move your feet a few boards to the right (if you're hooking it).

5. Follow Through

Your hand should follow the ball towards your target, and your arm should finish high, like you're answering a phone. A strong follow-through ensures the ball rolls cleanly off your hand.
