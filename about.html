About Us

Learn more about the heart and soul behind StrikeZone's family entertainment experience.

Our Journey

Founded in 2016, StrikeZone was built on a simple premise: create a welcoming, high-energy environment where families, friends, and colleagues can come together and make lasting memories. We noticed a gap in our community for a truly modern entertainment center, and we decided to fill it.

What started as a modest 12-lane bowling alley has evolved into a full-scale family entertainment complex featuring VIP lanes, a next-gen arcade, and a gourmet restaurant and bar.

Our History

Our Journey So Far

From a small bowling alley to a full-scale entertainment destination.

2016
Grand Opening

StrikeZone opens its doors with 12 state-of-the-art bowling lanes and a small snack bar, welcoming the community with open arms.

2018
Arcade & Bar Expansion

We expanded with 50+ arcade machines, a full-service bar, and upgraded to 24 premium lanes — doubling our entertainment capacity.

2021
VIP & Events Launch

Launched VIP private lane packages and a dedicated events hall, making StrikeZone the top venue for corporate events and birthday parties.

2026
10,000+ Happy Customers & Growing

Today, StrikeZone stands as the region's premier family entertainment center, with over 10,000 loyal customers and hundreds of events hosted annually.
